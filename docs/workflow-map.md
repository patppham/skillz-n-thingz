## Notes On Authorship

The authorship labels are practical rather than legal:

- `Mine` means the skill appears to be primarily your workflow design and wording.
- `Adapted` means it likely started from external patterns, tools, or prior skill formats but is now customized to your workflow.
- `External` means it is mainly a shared or third-party skill you use as part of your stack.

This repo is meant to show the full daily operating system you use, not only the skills that are fully original.
